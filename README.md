# Anub'arak Simulator
In the hit video game World of Warcraft: Wrath of the Lich King Classic, Anub'arak, the final boss of the Trials of the Grand Crusader raid will chase you around. If you don't know what to do, he'll kill you and wipe the raid and then everyone will be mad at you.

Instead, play [this minigame](https://play.unity.com/en/games/43ed87fe-072e-4d4d-ba80-d3a99b7e398e/wotlk-classic-anubarak-simulator-v121) to practice!
